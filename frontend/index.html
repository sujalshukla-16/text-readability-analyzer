How the result is calculated

The analyzer combines three standard readability metrics.

01

Flesch Reading Ease

Uses sentence length and syllables per word. A higher score generally indicates easier text.

02

Flesch-Kincaid

Estimates the education grade level required to understand the text.

03

Coleman-Liau

Uses average letters and sentences to estimate text complexity.
